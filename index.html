    Frequently asked questions
    What is the most important principle in interior design?
    Proportion and scale. Before colour or furniture, the relationship between objects and the human body determines whether a room feels comfortable. A well-proportioned empty room will always feel better than a poorly proportioned furnished one.
    How do I plan a renovation without an architect?
    Start with a measured floor plan, identify load-bearing walls, and establish a realistic budget with a 20% contingency. For structural changes or extensions, you will need an architect or engineer for permits and safety.
    What are the best lighting temperatures for living spaces?
    2700K–3000K for living rooms and bedrooms (warm, relaxing), 3000K–3500K for kitchens and bathrooms (clean, functional). Avoid 4000K+ in residential settings — it feels clinical.
    How can I make a small room feel larger?
    Use consistent flooring, low-profile furniture, and vertical storage. Avoid heavy curtains and dark corners. Mirrors help, but only when they reflect something worth seeing.
