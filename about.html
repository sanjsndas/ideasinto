    Ideas Into Architecture is an independent publication for people who want to understand how spaces work — not just how they look. We write about interior design, architecture, materials, and the decisions that shape daily life.

    Our story

    Ideas Into Architecture began in 2016 as a personal notebook. Elena Marchetti, a former architect, was frustrated by the gap between professional design knowledge and the information available to homeowners and enthusiasts. Too much of what she read was either superficial or needlessly opaque.

    So she started writing. The first articles were practical guides — how to read a floor plan, how to evaluate a material, how to plan lighting that actually works. They found an audience. Soon, other writers and designers joined. The notebook became a publication.

    Today, Ideas Into Architecture reaches hundreds of thousands of readers each month. We remain independent, reader-supported, and committed to the same principle: design knowledge should be rigorous, accessible, and honest.

    “A well-designed room doesn't announce itself. It simply makes your life easier, quieter, and more pleasant.”

    — Elena Marchetti, Founder

    What we believe

    We are not a trend blog. We are not a product catalogue. We are a publication for people who want to understand the why behind the what.

    Clarity over complexity

    Good design writing should be precise, not pretentious. We explain things clearly — no jargon for its own sake.

    Practical over theoretical

    We care about what you can actually use. Every article aims to leave you with a concrete insight or decision.

    Depth over speed

    We take time to research. We talk to makers, architects, and materials specialists. We don't chase algorithms.

    Independence over influence

    We are reader-supported. We don't accept sponsored content. Our only obligation is to our readers.

    How we work

    Every article is written by a human, edited by a human, and fact-checked before publication. We cite sources when appropriate. We acknowledge uncertainty when it exists. We correct errors promptly and transparently.

    Our editorial team includes architects, interior designers, and experienced journalists. We bring professional expertise to a general audience — without diluting the substance.

    Our contributors

    We work with a small group of regular contributors and guest writers. If you have expertise and a clear point of view, we'd like to hear from you. Get in touch.
